Workflow

How Passli Works

A straightforward three-step protocol.

STEP 01

Upload to your vault

Store personal documents in structured categories. Files are stored securely and kept private to your account.

STEP 02

Issue a Share Pass

Check only the specific files you want to present, set permissions and expiration, and generate a QR code with an access key.

STEP 03

Recipient access

The recipient scans the QR code, enters the key, and views only the selected documents. Access expires automatically.

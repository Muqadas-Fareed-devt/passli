Sharing Protocol

How Controlled Document Sharing Operates

A secure three-stage protocol designed for zero full-account exposure.

STAGE 01

Scan Protected QR

The recipient scans the QR code, directing them exclusively to an isolated endpoint URL with zero unauthenticated payload.

STAGE 02

Enter Share Key

The recipient supplies the separate 8-character key (e.g. 8K7P-42XM), which is verified server-side against a secure hash.

STAGE 03

Authorized Access

Access is granted strictly to the selected documents with owner-defined permissions (View-Only or Download) until expiration.
